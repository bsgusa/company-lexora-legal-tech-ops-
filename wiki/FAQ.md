## General

### What is Lexora AI?
Document-preparation and review software for legal and career paperwork. It
helps you organise information, draft text, and check documents. It is not a law
firm and does not provide legal advice or representation.

### Who uses Lexora AI?
We are early. We do not claim customers we do not have. If you want to be a
design partner, contact us.

### In what jurisdictions does Lexora AI work?
Documents can be tagged by jurisdiction and the review prompt adjusts. Coverage
grows as we validate it. We do not claim complete coverage of any legal system.

---

## Technology

### Is Lexora AI built on a proprietary model?
No. There is no “LexLLM”. We build retrieval, grounding, prompting, and review
around commercial models, called through the Balamonis AI Gateway.

### How accurate is Lexora AI?
We do not publish an accuracy figure. Accuracy depends on the document set and
the task, and any figure should come with the evaluation method behind it. We
will publish a method before quoting a number.

### How fast is it?
Speed depends on document length and the capability tier requested. We do not
quote fixed timings.

### Can it make mistakes?
Yes. All language models can. That is why a person reviews every output before
anything is filed, sent, or submitted.

## Security & data

### Does my data train your models?
We do not use customer documents to train models.

### Where is my data stored?
It depends on the deployment region. We will state the region in writing per
engagement.

### Is Lexora AI GDPR compliant?
We are working on readiness. We are **not** certified or independently audited,
and we do not claim compliance.

### Does Lexora AI preserve attorney-client privilege?
Handling privileged material requires per-matter isolation and legal review. We
do not claim automatic privilege protection. Privilege applies to communications
with a licensed attorney you engage — not to a document-preparation tool.
